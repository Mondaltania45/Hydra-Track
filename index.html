Hydration awareness: consider your conditions

Heat and sustained activity can increase fluid losses. Take regular breaks and follow your workplace heat-safety procedures.

This is a general awareness prompt, not a measurement of body-water status or a personalised medical prescription. It does not calculate a safe fluid dose.
